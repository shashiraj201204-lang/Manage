Security & device sessions

Website sign-in is remembered for up to 30 days on this browser/device using a secure, HTTP-only cookie.

For safety, every new browser/device must authenticate separately. A login on one device cannot securely make every unknown device logged in automatically.

Telegram login sessions are stored encrypted on the server. Use Telegram Settings → Devices to review or terminate Telegram sessions.

Never share your Telegram OTP, two-step password, API hash, or server encryption key.
